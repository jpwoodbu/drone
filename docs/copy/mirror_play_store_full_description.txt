<b>Drone Mirror</b> is specifically developed for the DJI Mavic Air (WM230) aircraft and its standard remote controller (model S01A). It is not known to be compatible with other aircraft models or remote controllers.

<b>OVERVIEW</b>

<b>Drone Mirror</b> is a low-latency viewer application that displays live camera video and flight telemetry relayed over your local Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot from the companion <b>Drone Bridge</b> app.

The DJI Mavic Air remote controller communicates over USB using the Android Open Accessory (AOA) protocol. Because Android-based VR headsets—such as Meta Quest—do not support AOA mode, they cannot connect directly to the controller. The companion <b>Drone Bridge</b> app solves this by running on an Android device plugged into the remote controller via USB, relaying the live video and telemetry stream over Wi-Fi to <b>Drone Mirror</b>.

Designed as a standard 2D flat Android application and available on both Google Play and the Meta Horizon Store, <b>Drone Mirror</b> is ideal for use with a VR headset in pass-through mode, floating like a display in front of you. By receiving video and telemetry wirelessly, it allows you to view your flight feed without tethering your display device directly to the physical remote controller.

<b>KEY MIRROR FEATURES</b>

* Low-Latency Video: Unprocessed video stream is passed through via <b>Drone Bridge</b> and decoded in hardware for lowest possible latency.
* Real-Time Telemetry HUD: Features an optional Heads-Up Display (HUD) overlay and a top status bar displaying altitude, speed, distance from home, battery percentage, GPS satellite lock, pitch/roll attitude, compass heading, and gimbal pitch.
* Diagnostics & Stream Health: The bottom status bar provides live stream metrics including video resolution, bitrate, framerate, recording duration, and network packet drop statistics.
* Detailed Diagnostic Dialogs: Tap metrics to inspect detailed readouts, including individual battery cell voltages, flight mode descriptions, camera settings, and real-time network connection health.
* Composition Grid Overlay: Quickly toggle a rule-of-thirds composition framing grid directly from the remote controller's C button.
* Automatic Discovery: Automatically locates the bridge device across your local Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot, with manual IP address configuration available as a fallback.
* Metric & Imperial Support: Seamlessly switch between metric and imperial flight measurement units with a single tap.
* No Virtual Controls: Flight control remains completely managed by the physical DJI remote controller. Neither <b>Drone Mirror</b> nor <b>Drone Bridge</b> provides virtual flight sticks on screen.
* Zero Data Collection: Drone Mirror does not collect, track, or transmit any telemetry, video, or personal data to the internet or third-party servers. All data remains strictly on your local network.

<b>REQUIREMENTS</b>

* Aircraft: DJI Mavic Air (WM230)
* Remote Controller: DJI S01A Remote Controller
* Companion App: <b>Drone Bridge</b> (available on Google Play) running on the Android device connected via USB to the remote controller
* Network: Both devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot
