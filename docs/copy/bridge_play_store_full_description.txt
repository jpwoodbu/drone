<b>Drone Bridge</b> is specifically developed for the DJI Mavic Air (WM230) aircraft and its standard remote controller (model S01A). It is not known to be compatible with other aircraft models or remote controllers.

<b>OVERVIEW</b>

<b>Drone Bridge</b> serves as a communication gateway between your DJI Mavic Air remote controller and the free <b>Drone Mirror</b> viewer app over your local Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot.

While standalone Android VR headsets—such as Meta Quest—provide a great platform for viewing flight telemetry and video, they do not support the Android Open Accessory (AOA) mode needed for the Mavic Air remote controller.

<b>Drone Bridge</b> bridges this gap. Your Android device connects to the USB port of the DJI remote controller, receives flight telemetry and video, and relays that data across your local Wi-Fi or hotspot to the companion <b>Drone Mirror</b> app (available for free on Google Play and the Meta Horizon Store) for it to display.

<b>KEY BRIDGE FEATURES</b>

* Real-Time Video & Telemetry Relay: Forwards live video and flight telemetry from the remote controller to the Drone Mirror app with minimal latency. Relayed data can also be previewed within the Drone Bridge app.
* Flight control is completely managed by the physical DJI remote controller: Neither <b>Drone Bridge</b> or its companion app, <b>Drone Mirror</b> provide virtual flight control sticks or any other kind of flight controls.
* Photos and Videos while in-flight: Use the DJI remote controller video and shutter buttons to take videos and photos while in-flight.
* Persistent Foreground Service: Keeps communication active even when the screen is turned off or you switch to another app.
* Live Diagnostics: Displays USB connection state and network statistics
* Zero Data Collection: Drone Bridge does not collect, track, or upload any telemetry or personal data. All data remains strictly on your local network.

<b>REQUIREMENTS</b>

* Aircraft: DJI Mavic Air (WM230)
* Remote Controller: DJI S01A Remote Controller
* Physical Connection: USB cable connecting your Android device to the USB port of the remote controller
* Network: Both devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot
* Companion App: <b>Drone Mirror</b> (available for free on Google Play and the Meta Horizon Store)
