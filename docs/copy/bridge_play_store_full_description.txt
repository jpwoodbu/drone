<b>DRONE COMPATIBILITY</b>

<b>Drone Bridge</b> was specifically developed for the <b>DJI Mavic Air</b> (WM230) aircraft and its standard remote controller (S01A). Compatibility with other aircraft models or remote controllers has not been tested firsthand by the author. However, testers have reported compatibility with the DJI Mavic Pro and the DJI Spark.

<b>OVERVIEW</b>

Use <b>Drone Bridge</b> together with the <b>Drone Mirror</b> app (free on the Meta Horizon Store) to view your drone's video feed and drone telemetry with a Meta VR headset (Android XR support coming soon).

<b>WHY TWO APPS?</b>

While standalone VR headsets—such as Meta Quest headsets—provide a great platform for viewing flight telemetry and video, they do not support the USB Android Open Accessory (AOA) mode needed for some DJI remote controllers.

<b>Drone Bridge</b> bridges this gap. Your Android device connects to the USB port of the DJI remote controller, receives flight telemetry and video, and relays that data across your local Wi-Fi or hotspot to the companion <b>Drone Mirror</b> app for it to display.

<b>KEY BRIDGE FEATURES</b>

* Real-Time Video & Telemetry Relay: Forwards live video and flight telemetry from the remote controller to the Drone Mirror app with minimal latency. Relayed data can also be previewed within the Drone Bridge app.
* Flight control is completely managed by the physical DJI remote controller: Neither <b>Drone Bridge</b> nor its companion app, <b>Drone Mirror</b>, provide virtual flight control sticks or any other kind of flight controls.
* Photos and Videos while in flight: Use the DJI remote controller video and shutter buttons to take videos and photos while in flight.
* Persistent Foreground Service: Keeps communication active even when the screen is turned off or you switch to another app.
* Live Diagnostics: Displays USB connection state and network statistics.
* Zero Data Collection: Drone Bridge does not collect, track, or upload any telemetry or personal data. All data remains strictly on your local network.

<b>REQUIREMENTS</b>

* Aircraft: DJI Mavic Air (WM230)
* Remote Controller: DJI S01A Remote Controller
* Physical Connection: USB cable connecting your Android device to the USB port of the remote controller
* Network: Both devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot
* Companion App: <b>Drone Mirror</b> (available for free on the Meta Horizon Store)
